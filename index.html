About mol-aspect

Calculate molecular aspect ratios from heavy-atom coordinates by PCA, and optionally view the principal axes in 3D.

Project page: https://github.com/pejpohno/mol-aspect How to cite: Ohno, M. mol-aspect. GitHub. https://github.com/PEJpOhno/mol-aspect (2025).
